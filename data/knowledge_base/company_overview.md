# Company Overview

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## About us

Juniper Lane Goods is an online shop founded in 2019 and based in Portland, Oregon. We sell homeware, small furniture, and small home electronics, chosen to be simple, durable, and fairly priced. We sell online only at juniperlane.example and have no physical stores or showrooms.

## Where orders ship from

All orders are packed and dispatched from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. We do not offer customer pickup from the warehouse.

## Contacting support

- Email: support@juniperlane.example
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm Eastern Time, excluding US public holidays
- Reply time: within 1 business day
- We do not offer phone support or support through social media.

Our automated assistant is available at any time for questions about policies and orders. It passes anything it cannot resolve to the human support team as a ticket.

## Order IDs

Every order has an ID in the form JL- followed by five digits, for example JL-10231. The order ID is shown in your order confirmation email and in your account under "My orders". Support needs the order ID, together with the email address used for the order, to look up an order.

## Stock and availability

Stock levels are shown on each product page. Support cannot reserve items, and we do not take back-orders or pre-orders. If an item is out of stock, you can sign up on its product page to be emailed when it returns.

## Personalised items

The Personalised Leather Notebook can be embossed with up to 20 characters. Personalised items are made to order, which adds 2 business days to the normal processing time, and they cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty or damaged.

## Business and bulk orders

We do not offer wholesale pricing or bulk discounts. Orders of more than 10 units of the same product may be cancelled if stock is limited; if that happens, the support team will contact you first.

## Newsletter

Newsletter subscribers occasionally receive discount codes. You can subscribe or unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any of our emails.
