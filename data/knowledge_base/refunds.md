# Refund Policy

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## Who approves refunds

Every refund is reviewed and approved by a member of our human support team. Refunds are never issued automatically, and our automated assistant cannot approve, promise, or issue a refund. If you ask for a refund, your request is passed to the support team, who will reply by email within 1 business day.

## When you can get a refund

You may be eligible for a refund when:

- You return an eligible item within the 30-day return window (see the Returns Policy).
- Your order arrived damaged or faulty.
- You received the wrong item and prefer a refund to a replacement.
- Your package is confirmed lost by the carrier.
- You cancelled your order before it was dispatched.

## How long refunds take

Once we receive your returned item, we inspect it within 3 business days. After the refund is approved, it is sent to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show it on your statement.

Refunds for cancelled orders that were never dispatched are sent within 3 business days of the cancellation.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method only. We cannot refund to a different card, a different person, or a bank account that was not used for the order. Orders paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit.

## What is and is not refunded

- The price of the returned item is refunded in full if it is unused and in its original packaging.
- The original shipping fee is refunded only if the item was faulty, damaged, or not what you ordered.
- A $6.99 return shipping fee is deducted when you return an item for any reason other than a fault, damage, or our mistake.
- Items returned used, or without their original packaging, may have a restocking fee of up to 20% of the item price deducted. The support team decides this after inspection.

## Partial refunds

If you return only some of the items in an order, only those items are refunded. If the remaining items no longer reach the $50.00 free-shipping threshold, we do not charge shipping after the fact.

## Refund disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to the refund email and a senior member of the support team will review your case within 3 business days.
