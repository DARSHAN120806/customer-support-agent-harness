# Shipping Policy

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only, including Alaska and Hawaii. We do not ship internationally, and we do not ship to PO boxes or freight forwarders.

## Shipping options and prices

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days after dispatch | $4.99, or free on orders of $50.00 or more |
| Express | 1–2 business days after dispatch | $12.99 on every order |

The $50.00 free-shipping threshold is based on the item subtotal before shipping. Express shipping is never free.

## Order processing time

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1 business day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm Eastern Time, or on a weekend or public holiday, are dispatched on the next business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays.

## Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched we email a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. Our support team can also tell you the status and tracking number of your order if you give them your order ID.

## Late or missing packages

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. If your tracking has not updated for 7 days or more, or your order is marked as delivered but you have not received it, contact support. A member of our support team will open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations usually take 3–5 business days. If the package is confirmed lost, the support team will offer you a replacement or a refund.

## Damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and photos of the packaging and the item. The support team will review the photos and arrange a replacement or a refund.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is "processing". Once an order has been dispatched, we cannot change the address. Address changes are made by the support team, not automatically.
