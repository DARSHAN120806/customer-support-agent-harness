# Frequently Asked Questions

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## What does Juniper Lane Goods sell?

We sell homeware, small furniture, and small home electronics, online only. We do not have physical stores.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Juniper Lane gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques, or cryptocurrency. All prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include GST.

## Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order while its status is "processing". Contact support with your order ID and the support team will cancel it. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled; you would need to return it after delivery instead (see the Returns Policy).

## Can I change the items in my order?

Items cannot be added, removed, or swapped after an order is placed. If the order is still "processing", you can ask support to cancel it and then place a new order.

## How do I track my order?

A tracking number is emailed to you when the order is dispatched. You can also ask support for the status of your order by giving your order ID.

## Do you price match?

No. We do not match competitors' prices, and we do not refund the difference if an item goes on sale after you buy it.

## Do you offer discount codes?

Discount codes are sent occasionally to newsletter subscribers. Only one code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed. Support cannot create or give out discount codes.

## How do gift cards work?

Gift cards are delivered by email, never expire, and can be used on any product. They cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged for cash.

## How do I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to you and is valid for 1 hour. Support cannot see or change your password.

## How do I update my email address or account details?

Sign in and go to Account Settings. For security reasons, support cannot change the email address on an account over chat.

## How do I contact a human?

Our human support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm Eastern Time, at support@juniperlane.example. If you ask our automated assistant for a human, it will create a support ticket and a team member will reply by email within 1 business day.

## Is my personal data safe?

We only discuss an order with the customer who placed it. Support will never share one customer's order, address, or contact details with another person, and will never ask for your password or full card number.
