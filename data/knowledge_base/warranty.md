# Warranty Policy

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## Warranty length

| Product type | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Electronics (speakers, earbuds, lamps, kettles, smart plugs) | 1 year from the delivery date |
| Furniture (tables, shelves, chairs) | 2 years from the delivery date |
| All other products | No warranty beyond the 30-day return window |

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing faults: a product that stops working, or has a defect in materials or workmanship, when used normally.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage, such as drops, spills, or cracks
- Water damage, unless the product is sold as waterproof
- Normal wear and tear, including scratches, fading, and reduced battery life over time
- Damage from misuse, unauthorised repair, or modification
- Products bought from anyone other than Juniper Lane Goods

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Contact support with your order ID, a short description of the fault, and photos or a short video showing it.
2. The support team reviews the claim within 3 business days.
3. If the claim is approved, we send a free return label. Once we receive the product we repair it, replace it, or, if neither is possible, refund it.

Warranty claims are always reviewed by a member of the support team. Our automated assistant cannot approve or reject a claim.

## Replacements under warranty

A replacement product is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period, or for 90 days, whichever is longer.

## Proof of purchase

Your order ID is your proof of purchase. The warranty applies to the original buyer only and cannot be transferred.
