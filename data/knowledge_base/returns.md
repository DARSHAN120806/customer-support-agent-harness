# Returns Policy

Juniper Lane Goods — last updated 1 September 2026

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days are counted from the day the carrier marked the order as delivered, not from the day you placed the order. Returns requested after 30 days are not accepted, except for faulty items covered by our warranty (see the Warranty Policy).

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging with all accessories and manuals. Items that come back used or without original packaging may be refused or may receive a reduced refund (see the Refund Policy).

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty or damaged:

- Gift cards
- Personalised or engraved items
- Candles and other scented products once the seal has been opened
- Items marked "final sale" on the product page

## How to start a return

1. Contact support with your order ID and the item you want to return.
2. The support team checks that the item is eligible and emails you a return label within 1 business day.
3. Pack the item securely, attach the label, and drop it off with the carrier within 14 days of receiving the label.

You can also start a return in the chat with our virtual assistant. A simple return of ₹5,000 or less (you changed your mind, or you received the wrong item) that meets this policy is approved straight away. Every other return is reviewed and approved by a member of the support team.

## Return shipping cost

- If the item is faulty, damaged, or not what you ordered, return shipping is free.
- For any other reason (for example, you changed your mind), a ₹699 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To swap an item, return it for a refund and place a new order.

## Wrong item received

If you received an item you did not order, contact support within 14 days of delivery. The support team will send a free return label and dispatch the correct item at no extra cost.
